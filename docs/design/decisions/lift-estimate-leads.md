# The e1RM estimate leads, with its range, from the first eligible set

Stage X, 9 Nov 2026. [e1rm.md](../e1rm.md) section 10 first said the estimate may lead only after a check on the
athlete's own data.

## Decision

The Lift card and page lead with the estimate and its 80% range as soon as a lift has an eligible set, as a
threshold or a race time does on the Run and Ride pages. The floor and the rep bests sit beside it as rows. There
is no gate on the athlete's own data.

## Why

- The planned check cannot do its job. It predicted reps through the same equations that made the estimate, so a
  lifter whose curve sits away from the equations cancels out (second science review on PR #482). The pairwise
  alternative reads effort under-reporting as curve shape (section 11). A sound check needs a fit of each lifter's
  own curve: research, not a gate.
- The range already carries the uncertainty the gate was for: the curve and centre terms in section 1 widen it
  for exactly the lifters the check would have flagged.
- The owner's direction (2 Nov 2026): lead with the best estimate, as Strava and Garmin do, and do not withhold an
  answer the data can give.

## Cost

If real lifters do fewer reps per load than the equations assume, the estimate reads low for them, and its range
holds the truth less often than 80% (section 7, centre 7: 74%).

## How to reverse

Hold the estimate behind the floor until a check exists, in the Lift card's lead line. No stored data changes.
