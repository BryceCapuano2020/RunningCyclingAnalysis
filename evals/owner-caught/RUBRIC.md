# Rubric

The owner scores each case on its own, pass or fail.

- **Pass**: the twin passes, and it would fail if the miss came back. The owner can name the code
  change that would break it.
- **Fail**: the twin fails, or it passes only because it stopped checking the thing: it asserts less than
  `case.md` says, loosens a tolerance, or drops a sport, a period or a library from its sweep.

The miss is back when:

- **home-screen-refusal**: a sport with years of history gets no answer on the Balance surface, or the
  surface prints a refusal or hedge where the answer should be.
- **two-thresholds-one-card**: two printed figures for one sport's threshold differ by more than their
  rounding.
- **threshold-slower-than-effort**: a threshold reads slower (run) or lower (ride) than the best hour in
  its period, old or isolated or not.
- **lean-vs-ride-as-a-run**: the lean names a sport that the ride as a run, set beside the run
  threshold, contradicts.
- **uncalibrated-anchor**: a world-class hour moves off its cited source by more than the stated
  tolerance.

A case with no twin that fails on the miss is not a case.
