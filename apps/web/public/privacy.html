RunningCyclingAnalysis

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026.

RunningCyclingAnalysis analyses your activity files in your browser. If you sign in, some of your data is stored on Cloudflare so it follows you between devices. This page says what is stored, where, for how long, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data. The app contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

Who operates the service

RunningCyclingAnalysis is operated by Bryce Capuano. The source code is public at GitHub. Privacy questions and requests go to the repository's issues. Do not put personal data, activity files or account details in a public issue. Say that you have a privacy request, and you will be asked for the minimum needed to act on it.

Without an account

Files you analyse without signing in are read and processed in your browser. They are not uploaded, and nothing from them is stored on our servers. The app saves your body mass, bike mass and unit preference in your browser's local storage. Nothing else is written there. You can clear them with your browser's site data controls.

With an account

You sign in with Google. Signing in uses only the basic openid, email and profile permissions. When you are signed in, each activity you analyse is saved to your library. We store:

  • Your account: the stable Google account identifier, your email address, your display name, and the profile picture address Google gives us. Your browser loads the picture directly from Google, and it is shown in the account menu.
  • Sign-in sessions: a one-way hash of your session token, your account, and when the session was created and expires. The token itself is kept only in your browser, in an HttpOnly cookie.
  • Profile settings: body mass, bike mass, your unit system, and whether you keep original files.
  • Activity summaries: for each saved activity, its name, start time and sport, the name and SHA-256 hash of the file it came from, its distance, duration and elevation gain, and the analysis summary computed in your browser. The analysis summary includes best efforts, heart-rate figures, climb sections and data-quality flags. It does not include the per-second recording or route coordinates.
  • Original activity files: stored only if you turn on Keep original files in my library. A file is uploaded only while this setting is on. Turning it off deletes the stored originals and keeps the summaries.

Google's own privacy policy governs what Google does with your account. We do not receive your Google password.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Account records, sessions, profile settings and activity summaries are stored in Cloudflare D1. Original files, when kept, are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. Cloudflare Workers serves the application and its interface. Cloudflare processes these data as our infrastructure provider. Google processes your sign-in under its own policies. When backups are enabled, encrypted copies are stored as GitHub Actions artifacts (see below). We do not sell your data, and we do not give it to anyone other than these providers.

Cookies and similar technology

The app uses only strictly necessary cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and short-lived cookies (ten minutes) that protect the Google sign-in. We set no tracking or advertising cookies.

Logs and rate limits

Cloudflare handles each request, including the network address it came from, under its own policies. Cloudflare uses that address for rate limiting, which the application does not store. When the application hits an unexpected error, it logs a request identifier and the error type. It does not log request bodies, file contents, tokens or query strings.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, activity summaries and original files stay until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Sessions end after 30 days without use, and in any case 90 days after they were created. Expired sessions are removed.
  • Application logs, which hold request identifiers and error types, are kept by Cloudflare for three days.
  • Backups are not enabled at the date of this page. When they are, each backup is an encrypted copy of the database, kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. A backup also holds a list of the stored original-file objects (their keys and sizes), but not the files themselves. Deleted data can remain in backups until those backups expire.

How to delete your data

  • Turn off Keep original files in my library to delete all stored originals while keeping your summaries.
  • Delete an activity from your library to remove its summary and any stored original file.
  • Choose Delete account in the account menu. This removes your account, sessions, profile, all activity summaries and all stored original files. Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups, if backups are enabled, until those backups expire.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. To use them, open a privacy request as described above. Deleting your account in the app is always available without a request.

Children

The service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

When this policy changes, the page is updated and its date changes too. Material changes to what is stored are described here before they take effect.
