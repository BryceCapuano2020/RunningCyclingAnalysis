Running Cycling Analysis Profile

Last updated 26 October 2026

Privacy policy

Analysis runs in your browser. Without an account, files are not uploaded. With an account, your profile and activity summaries are stored on Cloudflare, and original files only if you choose. We do not sell your data, and the app has no analytics or advertising trackers.

RunningCyclingAnalysis is run by Bryce Capuano, and its source is public on GitHub. Privacy requests go to the repository's issues. Do not put personal data in them.

Without an account

  • Files are analysed in your browser and not uploaded.
  • Each activity's summary is kept in this browser's IndexedDB where the browser allows, so results survive a reload. It holds the figures shown: times, splits per km and mile (flat-pace times, climb and heart rate), moving time, distances, heart-rate and power, average power, best efforts, climbs, power-meter calibration sums, the indoor flag and data-quality flags, and the file name. Its name comes from the activity list in a Strava export, else the title in the file, else the start time and sport. For an outdoor activity with GPS it also holds an outline of the route: the track with its position and scale removed, so it has no coordinates or scale. The outline is kept only in this browser and is never uploaded. The summary has no GPS coordinates, per-second data or original file, and it does not expire. When one file holds more than one activity, each of its summaries keeps a SHA-256 hash of that file, so they are shown together.
  • Settings are kept in this browser's local storage: weight, bike and gear weight, units, year and sex if given, whether setup is answered, and the view period you pick, under rca.period.v1. This copy is kept whether or not you have an account.
  • A flag in this browser's local storage, named rca.kept.v1, says whether this browser had results to show at its last visit, signed in or not, so a reload can show them at once. It holds no activity data.

Signing out does not remove these summaries, and they are not shown while you are signed in. Remove all my activities, offered on Profile only when signed out, removes them, as does clearing this site's data.

With an account

You sign in with Google, using only the openid, email and profile permissions. We never receive your password. Signed in, each activity you analyse is saved to your library, and we keep these until you delete them or delete your account:

  • Account: Google account ID, email, display name, and profile picture address (loaded from Google), and when the account was created.
  • Sign-in sessions: a one-way hash of your session token, your account, and its creation and expiry times. The token stays in your browser, in a sign-in cookie that lasts until your session ends. Sessions end up to 30 days after last use, and at most 90 days after they were created. Expired sessions are removed in daily cleanup.
  • Profile settings: weight, bike and gear weight, units, whether you keep original files, and year of birth and sex if given.
  • Activity summaries: name (from the activity list in a Strava export, the title in the file, or the start time and sport), sport, start and save times; file name and SHA-256 hash; distance, duration and elevation gain; and the analysis summary: times, splits per km and mile (flat-pace times, climb and heart rate), moving time, distances, heart-rate and power, average power, best efforts, climbs, power-meter calibration sums, the indoor flag and data-quality flags. No per-second recording or route data.
  • Original files: only while Keep original files in my library is on, and uploaded only then. Turning it off deletes them and keeps the summaries. A route outline is drawn from your original only when you ask for it, with Show route.
  • Daily counts: per account, downloads, saves, deletions, uploads, profile changes and library reads, kept for seven days.

Signed in, your settings are also kept in this browser's local storage, so signing out shows your latest values.

Storage, logs and backups

Accounts, sessions, settings, summaries, daily counts and the size of each kept original are in a Cloudflare D1 database, with short-lived rows for uploads in progress and for account deletions not yet finished. Original files are in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket, under keys that include your account and activity IDs. Summaries kept without an account stay in your browser, where it allows. Cloudflare hosts and processes this data as our provider, and Google processes sign-in. We do not sell your data.

Error logs hold a request ID, the error type and, for a saved activity that cannot be read, its ID. They never hold request bodies, files, tokens or query strings. Cloudflare's request logs include each request's network address, approximate location and browser user agent, and request URLs with the one-time Google sign-in code, which cannot be reused. The address is used only as a rate-limit key. Request and error logs are kept three days.

Cookies are strictly necessary only: the sign-in cookie, and two cookies that last ten minutes and protect the Google sign-in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

If backups are turned on, encrypted copies of the database are stored on GitHub, kept up to 90 days, then deleted. A backup also lists original-file objects, with keys and sizes, but not the files. Deleted data can remain in backups until they expire.

How to delete your data

On the Profile page you can:

  • Turn off Keep original files in my library to delete stored originals and keep summaries.
  • Delete one activity from Saved library, or from Kept in this browser when signed out. This removes its summary and any original file.
  • Choose Remove all my activities when signed out to remove every summary kept in this browser.
  • Clear your year of birth, or choose Prefer not to say for sex.
  • Choose Delete account. It removes your account, sessions, profile, activity summaries and original files. It does not clear this browser's copy of your settings or summaries kept here before you signed in, until you clear this site's data or use Remove all my activities while signed out.

Your data file

Signed in, Download my data on the Profile page gives one JSON file: its format and time, your account name and email, profile settings, and each saved activity's ID, sport, start, name, file name, whether an original is stored, save time and summary. It has no original files, and it says how many saved activities could not be read.

Rights and changes

You may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, which you can request as above. The service is not directed at children, and this page's date changes with the policy.
